The bundled `ChineseTraditional.isl` is the Traditional Chinese Inno Setup translation by GoneTone and the contributors listed in its header, obtained from the upstream Inno Setup repository:

https://github.com/jrsoftware/issrc/blob/main/Files/Languages/ChineseTraditional.isl

It is included locally because some Inno Setup 6 compiler installations do not bundle this language file. Attribution is preserved in the file header.
